About

I’ve spent the last 8+ years in healthcare. I started as a medical assistant, worked my way up to running operations across three offices for a behavioral health practice, and now I coordinate surgeries for a podiatry practice in Manhattan. Every one of those jobs came down to the same two things: keep patient data safe, and make messy processes actually work.

That’s how I ended up in security. I was already the one handling HIPAA, system access, and training every new hire on how to handle patient info, so I went and got the certs to back it up. The projects here are me getting hands-on with the technical side.

I also like building things that save people time. At work I made a tool that fills out our surgery booking sheets and figures out clearance dates so nobody has to do it by hand. Right now I’m finishing my bachelor’s at SNHU in business with a focus on I-O psychology.
